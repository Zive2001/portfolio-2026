04 process / how this usually goes
01

We talk.

30 minutes. No deck, no NDA theatre. I want to know what's actually broken and who feels it.

~30 min · free
02

Tiny proposal.

One page. Scope, milestones, fixed price or weekly retainer. You either nod or we walk away friends.

~3 days
03

Design + build, in public.

Live preview link from day one. Slack, Discord, smoke signals — pick a channel. You see every brushstroke.

2 — 8 weeks
04

Ship. Then keep shipping.

Production deploy, handover docs, optional retainer for the inevitable "one more thing." I don't disappear.

forever, ish
